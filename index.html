About

Dr. Changyi Ma joined the School of Artificial Intelligence at Jilin University (JLU) in 2025 as a tenure-track Associate Professor. She received her Ph.D. degree from The Chinese University of Hong Kong (QS #36, 2025), advised by Prof. Shuguang Cui (IEEE Fellow) and Prof. Wenye Li, and received the Presidential Award for Outstanding Doctoral Student of CUHK-Shenzhen 2022. Her research interests include Information Retrieval, Similarity Matrix Calibration, Continual Learning, and Large Language Models (LLMs). I am always looking for self-motivated PhD/RA/Visiting/Master students.
Please send me an email to query the detail: changyima@jlu.edu.cn.
